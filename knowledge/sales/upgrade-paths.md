# Upgrade Paths

## DIY to DWY Triggers

- Reconciliation remains incomplete.
- Multiple platforms require normalization.
- Material reserves or holds exist.
- The owner needs accountability or interpretation.

## DWY to DFY Triggers

- Monthly file volume becomes substantial.
- The business needs recurring close support.
- Multiple platforms, currencies, or accounts are involved.
- Management needs standardized reporting.
- Cash-timing and funding-readiness decisions are recurring.
