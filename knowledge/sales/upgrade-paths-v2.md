# Upgrade Paths v2

## Starter to Audit Lab

Recommend Audit Lab when the operator wants a recurring monthly audit, full templates, or a self-directed process after the initial document checklist.

## Audit Lab to Margin Control

Recommend Margin Control when reconciliation exceptions remain unresolved, multiple platforms require normalization, reserves or holds create an operational question, or the operator wants structured interpretation. This is a support recommendation, not a finding of financial risk.

## Margin Control to Guided Margin Recovery

Recommend Guided Margin Recovery when a private workspace, recurring data-gap management, material-exception review, or a more structured monthly cadence is needed.

## Guided Margin Recovery to Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk

Recommend Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk when multi-channel complexity, multiple currencies, high file volume, recurring close demands, or weekly cash pressure make guided work insufficient. The desk remains subject to a scoped service review.

## Any plan to Partner / Agency License

Discuss the Partner / Agency License only with a qualified organization serving multiple client businesses that accepts the licensing, privacy, and quality-control requirements. It does not authorize unapproved white-label claims or financial representations.

## Add-on route

When the need is a finite investigation rather than recurring membership, consider the One-time Platform Fee Leak Scan, Historical Reconciliation Sprint, Reserve and Payout Investigation, or SKU Margin Reset. Each has **placeholder** pricing until scope review.

## Conversation rule

Use observed workload and the operator's stated support need. Do not claim that an upgrade will recover fees, reduce costs, qualify the business for funding, or solve an accounting, tax, legal, or underwriting matter.
