# Objection Handling

## Planned Objections

- I already have a bookkeeper.
- Shopify, Amazon, or Stripe already gives me reports.
- I can do this in a spreadsheet.
- Why do I need a subscription?
- Why not use ChatGPT directly?
- Is this accounting or tax advice?
- Can this recover fees automatically?
- Will this help me qualify for funding?
- What data must I upload?
- How is sensitive information handled?
