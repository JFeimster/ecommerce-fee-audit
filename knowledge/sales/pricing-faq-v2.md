# Pricing FAQ v2

## Are these prices approved?

No. Every canonical amount is **provisional** until commercial approval. Placeholder prices are not quotes. Founding-member pricing is a separate, limited proposal and not canonical pricing.

## What is included in each plan?

Starter is a first-pass checklist. Audit Lab is self-service. Margin Control and Guided Margin Recovery add guided workflows and defined human-review support. Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk is scoped managed work. Partner / Agency License supports licensed partner delivery. See [the comparison matrix](../products/product-comparison-matrix-v2.md).

## Are annual plans available?

Provisional annual options are $190 for Audit Lab, $790 for Margin Control, $2,990 for Guided Margin Recovery, from $15,000 for Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk, and from $9,990 for Partner / Agency License. Annual terms and billing controls require approval before checkout is configured.

## Is there a setup fee?

Guided Margin Recovery and Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk may have a setup fee, but both are **placeholder** amounts until scope and source condition are reviewed. The separate founding-member proposal includes a $500 setup fee and remains subject to approval.

## What are the limits?

Operating limits are provisional: Starter supports one platform, Audit Lab two, Margin Control and Guided Margin Recovery three, while managed and partner work is scoped. Limits do not authorize services outside the agreed scope.

## Is historical cleanup included?

No recurring plan automatically includes it. Historical Reconciliation Sprint is a separately scoped add-on with **placeholder** pricing.

## Will you submit disputes, change books, or apply for funding?

No. The product can help organize documentation and readiness information, but it does not automatically submit disputes, alter accounting records, move money, or submit funding applications. Any external write needs explicit confirmation and applicable human review.

## Are ChatGPT, Wix, Notion, or other subscriptions included?

No, unless a future signed offer explicitly says so. Third-party access, availability, and pricing are outside this catalog.

## Can a customer change plans?

The intended path is Starter → Audit Lab → Margin Control → Guided Margin Recovery → Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk, based on complexity and support needs. A plan change is not an eligibility or underwriting determination and is subject to the then-approved commercial terms.
