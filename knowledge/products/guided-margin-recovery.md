# Guided Margin Recovery

## Target user and urgent problem

For an operator with recurring reconciliation gaps, data gaps, reserves, or margin questions who needs a private, guided operating cadence but retains responsibility for decisions.

## Promise

Guide recurring reconciliation, fee diagnosis, margin review, and corrective-action planning using an approved workspace and human review where material.

## Deliverables and onboarding

- Everything in Margin Control
- Private audit workspace
- Guided monthly audit cycle and data-gap review
- Material-exception review and corrective-action register
- Guided dispute-documentation and funding-readiness preparation, when relevant

Onboarding includes a source inventory, privacy/redaction confirmation, platform map, first-period baseline, and documented assumptions. The configurable setup fee is a **placeholder**, set only after scope review.

## Limits and exclusions

Provisional limits are three platforms, one audit period monthly, and 30 source files per period. No automatic dispute filing, platform outreach, money movement, accounting-entry change, tax/legal advice, or funding submission is included.

## Pricing and CTA

Canonical pricing is $299/month or $2,990/year, **provisional**. A separate **founding-member** offer proposes $299/month for three months plus $500 setup for the first ten approved members; it is not canonical. Apply for a guided scope review.
