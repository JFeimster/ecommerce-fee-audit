# Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk

## Target user and urgent problem

For multi-channel operators with recurring reconciliation, reserve, SKU-margin, cash-timing, or source-management complexity that exceeds guided self-service.

## Promise

Provide a scoped managed finance-operations process for recurring platform-to-bank reconciliation, platform-cost visibility, and cash-timing reporting. Findings remain subject to source quality and human review.

## Deliverables and onboarding

- Scoped platform settlement and payout-to-bank reconciliation
- Fee-variance, reserve-and-hold, SKU-margin, and cash-timing reports
- Exception register, monthly operations summary, and prioritized next actions
- Funding-readiness support package when requested

Onboarding includes a data inventory, source-condition review, scope definition, privacy/redaction controls, baseline reconciliation, and service schedule.

## Limits and exclusions

Platforms, audit cadence, source volume, and review cadence are scoped rather than assumed. The desk is not a bookkeeping firm, tax or legal adviser, lender, underwriting service, dispute-submission service, or autonomous financial operator.

## Pricing and CTA

From $1,500/month or $15,000/year, **provisional**. Setup fee is a **placeholder** until scope review. Use an application and invoicing workflow; do not enable self-service checkout before service design and commercial approval.
