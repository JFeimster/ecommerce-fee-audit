# Margin Control

## Target user and urgent problem

For operators whose reports exist but whose payout mismatches, fee changes, reserves, or margin signals need structured interpretation and accountability.

## Promise

Add guided review to a recurring audit process so material exceptions can be organized for the operator's decision and appropriate human review.

## Deliverables and onboarding

- Everything in Audit Lab
- Guided monthly workflow and group clinic access
- Limited review of material exceptions
- Guided fee-variance, reserve, and funding-readiness summary workflow

Onboarding confirms up to three platforms, one monthly audit period, and the source-file checklist. The operator remains responsible for data accuracy and approvals.

## Limits and exclusions

Provisional limits are three platforms, 20 source files per period, and limited human review. It does not provide a private workspace, managed reconciliation, accounting treatment, tax or legal advice, external platform contact, dispute submission, or funding approval.

## Pricing and CTA

$79/month or $790/year, **provisional**. Move to **Guided Margin Recovery** when a private workspace, recurring data-gap handling, or deeper guided monthly cadence is required. Request a guided-fit review.
