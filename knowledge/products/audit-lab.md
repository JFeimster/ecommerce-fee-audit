# Audit Lab

## Target user and urgent problem

For operators who can work with exports but lack a repeatable monthly method for payout reconciliation, fee variance, reserves, and SKU contribution margin.

## Promise

Help the operator run a disciplined monthly audit and turn authorized exports into an organized explanation of money movement and exceptions.

## Deliverables and onboarding

- Guided monthly close workflow
- Full template library and platform playbooks
- Reconciliation, reserve, and SKU-margin worksheets
- Self-directed funding-readiness checklist

Onboarding: choose up to two platforms, confirm source-file availability, and complete the first monthly workflow. The operator owns uploads, classification assumptions, and decisions.

## Limits and exclusions

Provisional limits are two platforms, one audit period each month, and ten source files per period. No human review, private workspace, external filing, bookkeeping, tax, legal, accounting, or underwriting advice is included.

## Pricing and CTA

$19/month or $190/year, both **provisional** and subject to approval before publication. Upgrade to **Margin Control** when exceptions remain unresolved, multiple platforms need interpretation, or reserve exposure needs guided review. Start the monthly audit workflow.
