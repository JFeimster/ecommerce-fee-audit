# Partner / Agency License

## Target user and urgent problem

For eligible agencies, consultants, bookkeepers, and funding-adjacent partners who need a controlled way to use selected audit workflows across client engagements.

## Promise

License approved materials and workflows to support partner-delivered ecommerce fee-audit services while preserving source-data, privacy, and compliance boundaries.

## Deliverables and onboarding

- Licensed templates and workflow access within the contracted scope
- Partner implementation orientation and approved positioning
- Usage reporting and quality-control expectations
- Optional partner training or implementation support when separately scoped

Onboarding verifies the partner, intended use, client-data handling, attribution, and any white-label boundary before access is granted.

## Limits and exclusions

The partner remains responsible for its client relationships, advice, approvals, and legal/compliance obligations. The license does not permit outcome guarantees, unapproved white labeling, autonomous external writes, financial advice, lending decisions, or use of customer data outside the agreement.

## Pricing and CTA

From $999/month or $9,990/year, plus usage, all **provisional**. Usage pricing is a **placeholder**. Request a partner qualification and licensing review.
