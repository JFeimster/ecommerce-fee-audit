# Free Platform Fee Audit Starter

## Target user and urgent problem

For an ecommerce operator who cannot yet explain the gap between platform sales, payouts, and bank deposits, and needs a safe first step before choosing a deeper workflow.

## Promise

Provide a structured first-pass checklist that may help identify missing evidence, likely reconciliation questions, and an appropriate next workflow.

## Deliverables and onboarding

- Platform and account inventory
- Preliminary payout, reserve, fee, and SKU-margin question set
- Minimum-document checklist
- Suggested self-service, guided, or managed path

Onboarding is self-directed: select one primary platform, one reporting period, and review the checklist. The Starter does not ingest live accounts or make external changes.

## Limits and exclusions

One platform, one audit period, and up to three source files are provisional operating limits. It does not include a completed reconciliation, human review, bookkeeping, tax or legal advice, dispute submission, funding application, or a promise of recovery or approval.

## Upgrade trigger and CTA

Move to **Audit Lab** when a recurring monthly workflow or full template library is needed. Start by completing the source-file checklist and documenting the platform-to-bank question.
