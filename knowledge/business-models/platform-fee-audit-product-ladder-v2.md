# Platform Fee Audit Product Ladder v2

The ladder is organized by responsibility: self-service work, guided work, and managed work. It is designed to help operators reconcile what happened; it does not guarantee recovery, savings, funding approval, or underwriting outcomes.

## Core path

1. **Free Platform Fee Audit Starter** — a no-cost diagnostic and document checklist.
2. **Audit Lab** — $19/month, **provisional**; a self-service monthly operating system.
3. **Margin Control** — $79/month, **provisional**; guided interpretation and accountability.
4. **Guided Margin Recovery** — $299/month, **provisional**; private-workspace guided implementation. Configurable setup fee is a **placeholder**.
5. **Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk** — from $1,500/month, **provisional**; scoped managed reconciliation and reporting.
6. **Partner / Agency License** — from $999/month plus usage, **provisional**; partner-delivered work under approved controls. Usage pricing is a **placeholder**.

Annual options are provisional: Audit Lab $190/year, Margin Control $790/year, Guided Margin Recovery $2,990/year, Finance Ops Desk from $15,000/year, and Partner / Agency License from $9,990/year. See [pricing-tiers.yaml](../../config/products/pricing-tiers.yaml).

## Founding-member offer

Guided Margin Recovery has a separate **founding-member** proposal: $299/month for three months plus a $500 setup fee, limited to the first ten approved members. It is not canonical pricing and needs written commercial approval before use.

## One-time add-ons

The One-time Platform Fee Leak Scan, Historical Reconciliation Sprint, Reserve and Payout Investigation, and SKU Margin Reset are scoped add-ons with **placeholder** pricing. They are not subscriptions and require a source-condition review before a quote.

## Boundaries

Self-service plans provide education, templates, and user-run workflows. Guided plans add structured review without taking over every finance-operations task. Managed work is explicitly scoped and remains subject to client data quality, approval, and human review. No tier includes autonomous money movement, accounting changes, dispute submission, or funding application.
