# Project Context

## Project Name

Ecommerce Fee Audit / AI Platform Fee Audit Copilot

## Core Problem

Ecommerce operators can show revenue while still losing cash through platform fees, payout lag, reserves, refunds, disputes, ad spend timing, financing remittances, and SKU-level margin leakage.

## Core Outcome

Help the operator answer:

1. Did the platform payout reconcile to the bank?
2. Which fees and deductions changed?
3. Which SKUs still produce contribution margin?
4. How much cash is trapped in reserves, holds, refunds, or timing gaps?
5. What should be fixed before seeking funding or scaling spend?

## Product Ladder

| Level | Offer | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| DIY | Fee Audit Lab | Operators who can run guided workflows themselves |
| DWY | Margin Recovery Desk | Operators who need guided implementation and review |
| DFY | Ecommerce Finance Ops Desk | Operators who want recurring managed reconciliation and reporting |

## Primary Audience

- Shopify store owners
- Amazon sellers
- Stripe-powered DTC sellers
- Marketplace sellers
- Ecommerce founders managing inventory and ads
- Operators preparing for working capital or inventory financing
- Funding brokers or ecommerce consultants serving seller clients

## Brand Voice

Direct, practical, fintech-aware, and operator-minded. Avoid generic AI hype and vague business jargon.

## Compliance Boundary

This project may support analysis, organization, documentation, and operational decision-making. It must not promise fee recovery, funding approval, income, accounting treatment, tax outcomes, or underwriting results.
