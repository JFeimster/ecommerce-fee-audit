02 / HOW THE AUDIT WORKS

MAKE THE
MONEY TRAIL
EXPLAIN ITSELF.

  1. 1

    Prepare safe source data

    Bring exports with dates, amounts, IDs, SKU details, and platform descriptions. Redact credentials and unnecessary personal information first.

  2. 2

    Normalize the messy parts

    Map columns, classify fees, and separate temporary cash restrictions from permanent costs.

  3. 3

    Reconcile and flag exceptions

    Connect payouts to deposits, then surface mismatches, timing differences, refunds, disputes, and unresolved adjustments.

  4. 4

    Decide what the problem actually is

    Use the resulting audit trail to identify whether you need margin action, data cleanup, cash timing fixes, or human review.
