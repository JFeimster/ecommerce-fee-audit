THE AUDIT ORDER

Do not debate profit until the money trail stops lying.

Platform dashboards are built to report activity. Operators need a control board that explains what became cash, what stayed temporary, and what quietly became permanent cost.

01

FIRST / CONTROL

Payout reconciliation

Match orders or charges to settlements, settlements to platform payouts, and payouts to bank deposits. Separate true discrepancies from timing noise.

  • Date-range and currency validation
  • Duplicate transaction detection
  • Payout-to-bank variance review
02

SECOND / CLASSIFY

Fee leakage

Sort permanent fees from refunds, taxes, reserves, financing deductions, reimbursements, and unresolved adjustments before calling anything “lost.”

  • Effective fee-rate analysis
  • Category and period variance
  • Unresolved adjustment queue
03

THIRD / ECONOMICS

SKU contribution margin

Calculate what remains after product cost, fulfillment, shipping, returns, advertising, and attributable platform costs—not just gross margin theater.

  • SKU and channel scorecards
  • Negative-margin flags
  • Refund and ad-spend drag
04

FOURTH / DIAGNOSE

Cash-timing diagnosis

Determine whether the pressure comes from margin, reserve timing, inventory cash conversion, payout delays, data quality, or working-capital structure.

  • Reserve and hold exposure
  • Inventory timing gaps
  • Funding-readiness implications

PRIVACY / REDACTION

Bring transaction evidence.
Leave the keys to the kingdom out of it.

Redact credentials and unnecessary personal information before uploading files. The public site and embed do not collect or transmit raw financial exports.

Never upload

  • Passwords or bank login credentials
  • API keys, session tokens, or authentication cookies
  • Full card numbers or CVV codes
  • Social Security numbers
  • Unredacted customer payment data
  • Anything unrelated to the audit

May be needed

  • Transaction and payout IDs
  • Order IDs, dates, and amounts
  • SKU and product-cost data
  • Platform descriptions and fee categories
  • Reserve, refund, dispute, and reimbursement records
  • Matching bank deposit references
PUBLIC-SITE BOUNDARY: No financial-file upload exists here. A secure, explicitly approved workflow would be required before this site could collect raw exports.
